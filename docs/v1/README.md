# ForgeDesMondes v1: what this product starts from

This product is ForgeDesMondes rebuilt by harness v2, from the roadmap of the v1 product
(`Laucans/ForgeDesMondes`). It starts from what the client validated in v1, converted with no
model (`IMPORT.md` gives the numbers and what was lost):

- `mock/` — the 9 pages and 188 elements of v1's mock-up (skeleton pages: v1's look was not
  carried over), `data/model.json` (33 entities, 12 aggregates), `surfaces.json`, `ports.json`.
- `docs/v1/rules.feature` — v1's 54 rules. Their criteria are prose: they are a reference for
  the rules writer, who rewrites the ones a milestone needs in the step vocabulary into `rules/`.
